航海的猫 · Sailing Cat

隐私政策 · Privacy Policy

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 21 日 · Effective: October 21, 2026

适用范围：iOS App「航海的猫 / Sailing Cat」及其小组件、实时活动、专注锁定（屏幕使用时间）扩展。

中文 · English

一句话：我们不收集你的个人数据。没有账号，没有广告，没有第三方追踪，App 也没有接收数据的后端服务器。

1. 我们不收集什么

  • 不要求注册、登录或填写任何个人资料。
  • 不收集姓名、邮箱、电话、位置、通讯录、照片、广告标识符（IDFA）或系统设备标识（IDFV/序列号）。
  • 不包含第三方统计、广告或追踪 SDK。
  • 不向开发者服务器发送专注记录、待办、渔获、装扮或设置。
  • 不读取你的屏幕使用时间历史，也不知道你屏蔽了哪些具体 App。

2. 只保存在你设备上的数据

以下数据用于 App 功能本身，默认只保存在你的设备上，不用于广告或用户画像：

  • 专注记录、待办、渔获图鉴、装扮、成就和设置。
  • 漂流瓶里写给自己的话。
  • 船上相机生成的图片（只有你通过系统分享面板保存或发送时，才会离开 App）。
  • 崩溃或卡死诊断报告（由 Apple MetricKit 生成，最多保留 10 份；只有你在设置中主动导出并分享时，才会离开设备）。

小组件、实时活动和专注锁定扩展通过 App Group 在本机共享必要的状态（例如当前计时和语言），这些状态不会离开设备。

3. iCloud 同步与备份（可选）

开启「设置 → 同步数据到 iCloud」后，上述数据会保存在你自己的 iCloud 账户中（Apple iCloud 键值存储），用于在同一 Apple ID 的多台设备间同步。

开启 iCloud 备份后，备份快照会保存在你自己的 iCloud 云盘中 App 的容器里（iCloud.com.sea.sailingcat），用于误删、换机或数据出错时恢复。

为了合并多台设备的存档，App 会生成一个随机标识，和存档一起保存在本机与你的 iCloud 中；它不包含设备型号、序列号或广告标识，也不能用来识别你。

  • 这些数据由 Apple 的 iCloud 服务处理和加密；我们无法访问、读取或恢复。
  • 你可以随时在 App 设置中关闭同步或备份。
  • 也可以在系统「设置 → Apple 账户 → iCloud」中管理存储。
  • 删除 App 不会自动删除 iCloud 中的数据。

4. 屏幕使用时间（专注锁定）

使用「专注锁定」时，App 通过 Apple 的 Screen Time（Family Controls）框架屏蔽你选择的 App。

  • 你选择的 App 以 Apple 提供的加密令牌形式保存在本机；App 无法得知具体是哪些 App，也不会读取你的使用记录。
  • 该功能只用于你自己设定的专注时段，不提供家长监控或他人监控功能。
  • 你可以随时在系统「设置 → 屏幕使用时间」中撤销授权。

5. 动作传感器与通知

动作传感器只在本机实时用于让甲板上的小物件随手机倾斜滚动，不保存、不上传。

专注结束、漂流瓶到达等提醒由系统在本机安排，不经过我们的服务器。你可以随时在系统设置中关闭通知。

6. 船上相机与分享

照片和鱼拓卡片在你的设备上生成。App 不会读取你的相册，也不会自动上传；只有你主动通过系统分享面板保存到相册或发送给朋友时，图片才会离开 App。

7. App 内购买

订阅和买断由 Apple App Store 处理。我们不会获得你的支付卡号、账单地址或其他支付信息。

我们可能从 Apple 收到汇总的销售和订阅状态数据，用于了解产品表现；这些数据不包含你的个人身份信息。订阅会自动续订，可随时在系统「设置 → Apple 账户 → 订阅」中取消。

8. 儿童隐私

App 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。App 不是专门面向儿童设计，也不包含账号、聊天、广告或外部社交功能。

9. 你的权利与数据删除

  • 本机数据：打开 App 内「设置 → 关于航海的猫 → 重新开始」清除，或直接删除 App。
  • iCloud 同步数据：开启同步时，「重新开始」也会清除 iCloud 中的同步数据；也可以关闭同步。
  • iCloud 备份：在「设置 → iCloud 备份」中逐份删除。
  • 开发者侧数据：由于我们不收集个人数据，通常没有可供访问、更正、导出或删除的开发者侧数据。

如果你的司法辖区（例如欧盟 GDPR 或加州 CCPA/CPRA）赋予你访问、删除、更正或限制处理个人数据的权利，你仍然可以联系我们；由于我们不收集个人数据，这些权利通常没有可执行的对象。我们不会出售或分享你的个人信息。

10. 数据安全

本机数据受 iOS 沙盒和设备密码保护；iCloud 数据受 Apple 的加密和账户安全保护。我们不会出售、出租或分享你的数据，因为我们不收集这些数据。

11. 第三方服务

  • Apple App Store：处理 App 内购买。
  • Apple iCloud：提供可选的同步与备份。
  • GitHub Pages：仅托管本支持页面和隐私政策页面，不接收 App 数据。访问本页面时，GitHub 可能按其隐私政策记录常规访问日志（例如 IP 地址），这不受我们控制。

除上述服务外，App 不集成第三方 SDK。

12. 政策更新与联系

本政策更新时会修改页面顶部的生效日期；重大变更会在 App 内或本页显著说明。继续使用 App 即表示你接受更新后的政策。

In one sentence: we don't collect your personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no third-party tracking, and no backend server receiving data from the app.

1. What we don't collect

  • No sign-up, login, or personal profile.
  • No name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, advertising identifier (IDFA), or system device identifier (IDFV/serial number).
  • No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • No focus records, tasks, catches, outfits, or settings sent to a developer server.
  • No access to your Screen Time history, and no knowledge of which specific apps you shield.

2. Data kept only on your device

The following is used for the app's features, stays on your device by default, and is never used for advertising or profiling:

  • Focus sessions, tasks, your fish book, outfits, achievements, and settings.
  • Notes you write to yourself in drift bottles.
  • Images created with the on-board camera (they leave the app only when you choose to save or share them through the system share sheet).
  • Crash and hang diagnostics generated by Apple MetricKit (up to 10 reports; they leave the device only when you export and share them from Settings).

Widgets, Live Activities, and the Focus Lock extensions share necessary state on-device through an App Group (for example, the current timer and language). That state does not leave your device.

3. iCloud sync and backup (optional)

If you turn on Settings → Sync data to iCloud, this data is stored in your own iCloud account (Apple iCloud key-value storage) to keep devices signed in to the same Apple ID in sync.

If you turn on iCloud backup, backup snapshots are stored in your own iCloud Drive, in the app's container (iCloud.com.sea.sailingcat), so you can restore after an accidental deletion, a new device, or a data problem.

To merge saves from multiple devices, the app generates a random identifier and stores it with your save on this device and in your iCloud. It contains no device model, serial number, or advertising identifier, and cannot be used to identify you.

  • This data is handled and encrypted by Apple's iCloud service; we cannot access, read, or restore it.
  • You can turn sync or backup off at any time in the app's settings.
  • You can also manage storage in iOS Settings → Apple Account → iCloud.
  • Deleting the app does not automatically delete data in iCloud.

4. Screen Time (Focus Lock)

Focus Lock uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) framework to shield the apps you choose.

  • Your selection is stored on-device as opaque tokens provided by Apple; the app cannot see which apps they are and never reads your usage history.
  • It is used only for focus sessions you set for yourself. It does not provide parental or third-party monitoring.
  • You can revoke permission at any time in iOS Settings → Screen Time.

5. Motion sensors and notifications

Motion data is used on-device, in real time, to let deck items roll as you tilt your phone. It is never stored or uploaded.

Session-end and drift-bottle reminders are scheduled locally by iOS and do not pass through our servers. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.

6. On-board camera and sharing

Photos and fish-rubbing cards are created on your device. The app does not read your photo library or upload images automatically; an image leaves the app only when you choose to save it to Photos or send it to someone through the system share sheet.

7. In-app purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by the Apple App Store. We never receive your card number, billing address, or other payment information.

We may receive aggregated sales and subscription-status data from Apple to understand product performance; it does not identify you. Subscriptions renew automatically and can be canceled at any time in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

8. Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It is not specifically directed to children and contains no accounts, chat, advertising, or external social features.

9. Your rights and deleting data

  • On-device data: open Settings → About Sailing Cat → Start Over in the app, or delete the app.
  • iCloud sync data: when sync is on, Start Over also clears the synced iCloud data; you can also turn sync off.
  • iCloud backups: delete individual backups in Settings → iCloud Backup.
  • Developer-side data: because we do not collect personal data, there is generally no developer-side data to access, correct, export, or delete.

If your jurisdiction (for example, the EU GDPR or California CCPA/CPRA) gives you rights to access, delete, correct, or restrict processing of personal data, you can still contact us; because we do not collect personal data, those rights generally have no data to act on. We do not sell or share your personal information.

10. Data security

On-device data is protected by the iOS sandbox and your device passcode; iCloud data is protected by Apple's encryption and account security. We do not sell, rent, or share your data, because we do not collect it.

11. Third-party services

  • Apple App Store: processes in-app purchases.
  • Apple iCloud: provides optional sync and backup.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this support and privacy page only and receives no app data. When you visit this page, GitHub may keep standard access logs (such as your IP address) under its own privacy policy; this is outside our control.

Other than these services, the app includes no third-party SDKs.

12. Changes and contact

When this policy changes, we update the effective date at the top of the page; material changes will be explained in the app or prominently on this page. Continuing to use the app means you accept the updated policy.
